CALQENTRA Privacy Policy

Engineering Calculator & Reference Application

1. Introduction

CALQENTRA ("CALQENTRA", "we", "us", or "our") is an engineering calculator and reference application designed to provide engineering calculations, technical reference information, project functionality, and related tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and processed when you use the CALQENTRA application.

By using CALQENTRA, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Account Information

If you create a CALQENTRA account, we may process information required to create and maintain your account, such as your email address, authentication identifiers, and information associated with your account.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, authentication information provided through Google's authentication services may be processed to authenticate your account.

Calculator and Project Information

Depending on the features you use, CALQENTRA may store information associated with your calculator usage, saved projects, calculation history, favorites, and related application settings.

Purchase and Subscription Information

CALQENTRA may process information necessary to determine entitlement to paid or Pro features purchased through Google Play.

Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. CALQENTRA does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card information.

Analytics and Diagnostic Information

CALQENTRA may use analytics and crash-reporting services to understand application usage, diagnose technical problems, improve reliability, and improve the application.

Advertising Information

The free version of CALQENTRA may display advertisements. Advertising services such as Google AdMob may process device or advertising information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • provide calculator and engineering-reference functionality;
  • save and synchronize user projects and related information;
  • provide saved calculations, history, and favorites;
  • provide and verify Pro features and purchases;
  • diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • understand application usage and improve CALQENTRA;
  • display advertisements in the free version; and
  • maintain the security and reliability of the application.

4. Third-Party Services

CALQENTRA may use third-party services to provide authentication, cloud storage, application functionality, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and Google Play purchase processing.

These services may include:

  • Google Firebase
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Sign-In

These providers may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Account and application data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide CALQENTRA's services and maintain the user's account.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account data will be deleted in accordance with the applicable deletion process, except where retention is required or permitted for legitimate purposes such as legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Certain purchase, transaction, security, or diagnostic records may be retained by third-party service providers according to their own policies and legal obligations.
